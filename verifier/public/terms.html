What a result means

A verification result is evidence about a file, not a judgment about a person. It tells you whether the receipt's contents still agree with the signature over them, and whether the text you supplied matches the one the receipt describes. It does not establish who holds the signing key, that a human rather than a pipeline wrote the piece, or that the recorded process reflects honest work. The limits are stated on the verifier itself and in section 9 of the specification.
