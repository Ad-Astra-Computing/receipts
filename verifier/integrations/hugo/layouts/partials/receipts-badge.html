{{- /* Folio "Receipts of thought" badge. Drop into your Hugo theme and call {{ partial "receipts-badge.html" . }} in single.html where you want the badge. It gives a reader the post's receipts bundle and a link to the verifier that checks it. The badge deliberately does NOT hand the verifier a URL to fetch. The verifier never makes a network request: its content-security policy forbids one, which is what lets it promise that a bundle you check never leaves your machine. So the reader downloads the bundle and drops it on the verifier, which is two clicks and no trust in us. Point it at the bundle with a `receipts` page param, or publish the bundle beside the post as `receipts.json` (the default). */ -}} {{- $receipts := .Params.receipts | default (printf "%sreceipts.json" .Permalink) -}} {{- with $receipts -}} ◆ Receipts of thought verify it {{- end -}}
